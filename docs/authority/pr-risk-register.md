# Digital PR Risk Register

**Date:** 2026-09-14 · Reviewed each campaign, and before any outreach begins.

Risks are rated by what they would cost if realised. In this market the expensive failures are
not "a campaign underperformed" — they are claims that turn out to be wrong, and outreach that
makes the brand look like the spam it is trying to differentiate from.

| #   | Risk                                                | Severity | Status     | Control                                                                                                                                    |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| R1  | Outreach sent while the site does not resolve       | CRITICAL | **ACTIVE** | No outreach until launch. A dead link permanently burns the recipient relationship.                                                        |
| R2  | Publishing a result date or fee that proves wrong   | CRITICAL | Controlled | Every volatile fact carries source + date + confidence; nothing unsourced ships. Freshness gate fails the build on a passed expected date. |
| R3  | Student data used as a PR asset                     | CRITICAL | Controlled | No personal result URL exists anywhere on the site. Prohibited in the editorial policy and the strategy.                                   |
| R4  | Implying affiliation or endorsement by a board      | HIGH     | Controlled | `/about` states independence explicitly; footer disclaimer sitewide; no board branding used.                                               |
| R5  | An asset URL moves after links are earned           | HIGH     | Controlled | Stable URLs; yearless board pages; rollover doc forbids redirecting historical URLs to a new year.                                         |
| R6  | Overgeneralising from a small dataset               | HIGH     | Open       | Six boards is six boards, not "Pakistan". Every claim states its scope. Review before any pitch.                                           |
| R7  | Outreach reading as spam and damaging the domain    | HIGH     | Open       | No mass sending, no templates, personalised only, restrained follow-up, declines respected permanently.                                    |
| R8  | Copyright — charts, screenshots, board logos        | MEDIUM   | Open       | Original charts only, from data we may lawfully use. Board logos not used. Competitor visuals never reproduced.                            |
| R9  | A stale report still circulating as current         | MEDIUM   | Controlled | Annual reports are dated and archived, never renamed into a new year.                                                                      |
| R10 | Third party misrepresents our data in coverage      | MEDIUM   | Open       | Publish methodology alongside every dataset; correct politely where it matters.                                                            |
| R11 | A competitor link tactic copied without classifying | LOW      | Controlled | Strategy requires classifying each competitor link before treating it as an opportunity.                                                   |

---

## R1 is the one that matters today

Everything else is a control that already exists or a discipline to hold during a campaign. R1 is
live and unmitigated except by not acting: **the site does not resolve**, so any outreach now
would send people to nothing.

It clears when the Cloudflare zone is resolved and the site is serving on its apex.

---

## Claim review, before anything is pitched

Every claim in a pitch or PR asset must survive these, in order:

1. Is it supported by a primary source we hold?
2. Is the scope stated — how many boards, which cycle, which examination?
3. Are we implying causation from correlation?
4. Is the source still current, and when was it last checked?
5. Is the wording sensational anywhere it could simply be accurate?

If any answer is uncomfortable, the claim is softened or dropped. Coverage earned on a claim that
later fails costs more than the coverage was worth — and on this site, being right is the entire
proposition.
