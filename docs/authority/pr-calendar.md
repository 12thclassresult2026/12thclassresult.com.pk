# Digital PR Calendar

**Status:** planning only. No campaign runs until the site is live — see R1 in the risk register.

Seasonal editorial demand in this market is genuine and predictable, which means the work is
preparation rather than invention. Nothing here manufactures news to fill a slot.

---

## The cycle

| Period                      | What is actually happening                    | Asset that becomes relevant                               |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Exam season                 | Candidates sitting papers; date-sheet queries | Board directory; date-sheet guidance (not built)          |
| **Result announcement**     | **Peak editorial demand**; boards declare     | Board status data; historical date archive; method matrix |
| Immediately post-result     | Rechecking and second-annual questions surge  | **Rechecking comparison**; percentage explainer           |
| Rechecking window           | 15-day deadlines run at several boards        | Rechecking comparison — highest utility here              |
| Second annual / improvement | Registration windows open                     | Second annual guide (not built)                           |
| Admissions season           | Post-HSSC transition                          | Percentage explainer; aggregate guidance (rejected)       |
| New academic year           | Quieter; research and archive work            | Annual review; gazette index                              |

Sindh matters separately: Karachi declares **group by group**, weeks apart. A single national
"result day" framing is wrong there, and that difference is itself the story.

---

## Result season preparation, in order

Begin before the season, not during it.

1. Re-verify every board source; update check dates
2. Refresh the historical date archive with the previous cycle's actual dates
3. Verify the result-method matrix board by board
4. Prepare a plain data summary a journalist can use without interpreting it
5. Identify relevant outlets — national education desks, and regional outlets for their own board
6. Targeted, personalised outreach only
7. Keep assets updated _during_ the cycle; a stale status page in result week is worse than none
8. Log every mention and link

## Regional angles

Board results are inherently local. A regional outlet cares about its own board, so the pitch is
that board's data — not a national roundup.

## What is never done here

- No invented news to fill a calendar slot
- No exploiting a tragedy or a sensitive event for coverage
- No press-release syndication for backlinks
- No pitching during result day itself with anything unverified — the one moment accuracy matters
  most is the one moment a rushed claim does the most damage
