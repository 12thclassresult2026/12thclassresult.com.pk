**Phase:** 1 — Research
**Date:** 2026-09-14

> **Nothing here is a claim of superiority.** Nothing has been published or measured.

> what was actually observed in the market.

---

## 1. The one advantage that is not copyable

Every competitor advantage in this market can be copied in an afternoon — layout,
tables, FAQ counts, a countdown, even a board list.

**Verification cannot.** It requires loading board sites, recording what was seen,
distinguishing confirmed from tentative, and re-checking on a cadence.

The market's own contradictions prove nobody is doing it:

| Fact                                 | What the market publishes                                                             |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Punjab HSSC Part-II 2026 result date | **18, 23 and 13 September** — three different dates, none citing a board notification |
| One board's SMS shortcode            | **800291** and **80029** on different sites                                           |
| Another board's shortcode            | **800292** and **800299**                                                             |
| Federal board result date            | **9 September** and "expected 23 August"                                              |
| One aggregator's own page            | prints a shortcode while a note beside it says a different one is correct             |

Meanwhile, our own check of every board's own domain on the same day found **no HSSC
Part-II 2026 announcement at all** for Punjab, KPK, the Federal board or AJK.

**Target advantage:** be the only source that states what is actually confirmed, links
the document, and shows when it was last checked.

## 3. Structural opportunities nobody has taken

### 3.1 Boards that do not fit the Punjab model

Every competitor encodes "one board = one roll-number form = one date". Against that:

- **Karachi declares group by group**, up to four weeks apart, with **no roll-number lookup at all** — gazette PDFs only. Commerce was still undeclared when everyone else had "announced" the result.
- **Hyderabad publishes per-group results with no dates whatsoever.**
- **AJK's HSSC route is the gazette**, not a form.
- **Several KPK portals expose one session at a time**, so a "latest result" link points at a different class next month.

**Opportunity:** be the only site that is _correct_ about roughly a fifth of the country.

### 3.2 Rechecking — a genuine vacuum

Every engine returns Indian content for this family. Pakistani coverage is unsourced
ranges, and boards bury the detail in PDFs.

We already hold **real, board-published figures** — one KPK board publishes
re-totalling and show-of-paper fees per paper, with normal and late tiers and a closing
date. **Opportunity: the first sourced, per-board rechecking reference in Pakistan.**

### 3.3 Percentage calculation — the market is actively wrong

The ranking advice applies a foreign board's CGPA formula, which **does not apply to
Pakistan**, where HSSC marks are out of 1100. **Opportunity: be correct.**

### 3.4 "Result without roll number"

The weakest surface observed in the entire study — video, Q&A and short-form UGC only,
no authoritative answer. **Opportunity: answer it properly**, framed as roll-number
recovery, never as name lookup.

### 3.5 Urdu

Urdu-script results are ~100% Pakistani and served entirely by newspapers — **zero
aggregators, zero tools, zero boards** — and every Urdu roll-number how-to that ranks
is for matric, not intermediate. **Opportunity: an uncontested surface.**

### 3.6 Answer engines cite whoever states the fact plainly

Board sites are structurally unciteable — CAPTCHAs, PDF-only facts, JS portals, no
schema — so answer engines quote aggregators instead. **Opportunity: be the source
that is correct, dated and linked, in crawlable HTML.**

## 4. Where we should NOT compete

Recording these matters as much as the opportunities.

| Area                                                     | Why not                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Certificate verification / attestation / equivalence     | The official body owns half its own first page plus its portals                                                                                                                                                                |
| Duplicate DMC as a transaction                           | Officials and their own forms win it                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Entry-test aggregate calculators                         | Crowded, settled formula, established tools                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Position holders                                         | No official source exists for the current session                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Any result "checker" that claims to return marks in-page | **No first-party checker exists in this market**, and our own feasibility study shows why: six confirmed CAPTCHAs across five boards, VIEWSTATE-protected forms, JS-only portals, and no board publishing an API or permission |

---

## 5. Practices we will not adopt, whatever the traffic cost

Each was observed live:

- Countdowns to unconfirmed dates, or countdowns that ship as zeros
- "Updated on: [today]" auto-stamps
- Synthetic verification delays before an outbound link
- Board-impersonating subdomains or naming
- Publishing SMS shortcodes that cannot be traced to a board page
- Tools that fabricate result cards, or hosting students' scanned documents
- Group pages that are one template with a word swapped

---

## 6. The honest summary

**We are not better today. Nothing is published.**

What the evidence supports is narrower and more useful: this market is wide open on
**accuracy, provenance, non-Punjab correctness and post-result depth**, and those are
exactly the areas where the work is unglamorous and therefore undone.

The differentiator is not a feature. It is the willingness to write "not verified" when
that is the truth — on the one morning of the year when a student needs the answer.
